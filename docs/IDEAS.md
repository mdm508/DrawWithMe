# Idea log

These ideas are intentionally outside the current v1 commitment. Moving an item into v1 requires a project-management decision and an accompanying architecture update.

## Additional game modes

- **Gallery Vote:** everyone draws the selected prompt, then anonymously votes for a favorite.
- **Trace Race:** competitors trace a supplied line drawing; scoring combines path accuracy and speed.
- Telephone/relay modes that alternate prompts, drawings, and interpretations.
- User-created private word packs.

## Drawing expansion

- Paint bucket with bounded-region filling.
- Shapes, ruler, layers, stickers, textured tools, smudge, and highlighter.
- Saved gallery and shareable replay.
- Pressure/tilt brush variants and Pencil hover previews.

## Social and discovery

- Public rooms and public matchmaking.
- Optional audio rooms.
- Parties independent of an active game.
- Friends, recent players, reactions, achievements, and seasonal events.

## Progression and monetization

- Cosmetic brushes, palettes, canvas themes, profile frames, and podium effects.
- Unlockable word packs and host-owned rule packs that benefit the whole room.
- Ethical supporter purchase with no pay-to-win advantage.

## Platform growth

- SharePlay launch from FaceTime and Messages.
- Game Center leaderboards and challenges.
- Widgets, Live Activities, and visionOS investigation.
- Non-Apple clients only after the native Apple product is validated.
